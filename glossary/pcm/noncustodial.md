# Non-Custodial

**Category:** Security

## Plain English

Na Bitcoin wallet where you dey hold your own private keys and you get complete control of your funds, nobody else fit access or freeze your Bitcoin.

## Analogy

Non-custodial wallet na like keeping cash for home for safe. You dey control everything. Nobody fit lock you out, freeze your funds, or disappear with your money. But you dey also responsible for the safety of the safe and the keys.

## In Context

I dey use non-custodial wallet, I dey hold all my private keys myself.

What this means: You go download wallet app (like BlueWallet, Sparrow, or Ledger), generate private key, and now you alone go get the private key. No company dey involved. You fit send and receive Bitcoin completely independently.

## Why It Matters

Non-custodial na Bitcoin core value proposition: true ownership and control. No government fit freeze your account. No company fit lock you out, no censorship dey possible. If you lose the private key, you lose the Bitcoin forever, but that na the tradeoff for complete freedom. That na why serious Bitcoin users dey prefer non-custodial wallets.
