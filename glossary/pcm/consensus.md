# Consensus

**Category:** Network

## Plain English

Na agreement among the majority of nodes and miners on wetin the valid state of blockchain be, without any central authority wey go make that decision.

## Analogy

Imagine town where everybody dey keep their own copy of town records. Before new record get add, most townspeople must agree say e dey legitimate. Bitcoin dey work the same way, thousands of independent computers (nodes) must agree say transaction dey valid before e become permanent.

## In Context

Bitcoin dey reach consensus through proof of work.

What this means: Miners dey compete to solve puzzles and earn the right to add block. Once block get add, nodes dey verify that block. If 51% or more of network computing power agree say block dey valid, consensus dey reach and the block become part of the permanent record.

## Why It Matters

Consensus na wetin dey make Bitcoin decentralised and trustless. No single authority fit change the rules or rewrite history. One attacker go need to control more than 51% of network computing power to lie, na task so expensive and difficult say e no dey worth trying. Na why Bitcoin dey secure.
