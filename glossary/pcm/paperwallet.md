# Paper Wallet

**Category:** Security

## Plain English

Na Bitcoin wallet wey dey store entirely for paper, your private key and public address dey print, making am offline and immune to hacking.

## Analogy

Paper wallet na like writing down your house key and address for piece of paper and storing am for safe place. No computer fit hack am because e no dey digital. But if you lose the paper or somebody thief am, your funds dan go.

## In Context

I generate paper wallet, print am, and store am for safe deposit box for long-term savings.

What this means: You use tool generate private key, print both the key and address for paper, destroy the digital copy, and now you get completely offline Bitcoin storage method.

## Why It Matters

Paper wallets dey excellent for large amounts of Bitcoin you plan to hold for years. Because e dey offline, na one of the safest ways to store Bitcoin, no hacking, no malware, no loss of access. The downside na say spending dey take extra work: you must import the private key into digital wallet before you fit send Bitcoin.
