# Custodial

**Category:** Security

## Plain English

Na Bitcoin service where third party (like exchange or bank) dey hold your private keys and dey control your Bitcoin for your behalf.

## Analogy

Custodial wallet na like keeping money for bank. You no dey hold the money yourself, the bank dey do am. You dey trust the bank to give your money back when you ask. If the bank get hacked or go out of business, your money fit dey at risk.

## In Context

I dey keep my Bitcoin for exchange, that na custodial setup.

What this means: Coinbase, Kraken, and other exchanges dey custodial services. Dem dey hold your Bitcoin and your private keys. You fit access your funds through their website or app, but you no dey actually control the private keys.

## Why It Matters

Custodial services dey convenient for beginners, but dem dey sacrifice one of Bitcoin core benefits: self-sovereignty. You dey trust one company to secure your money and no disappear. Na famous saying for Bitcoin: "Not your keys, not your coins." If you wan get full control and security of your Bitcoin, move am to non-custodial wallet.
