# Channel

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

Na direct connection between two Lightning Network users wey dey let them send and receive Bitcoin back and forth instantly without touching the main blockchain.

## Analogy

Lightning channel na like tab for pub. You and the bartender dey open tab (one channel), then you fit order drinks and the bartender dey mark am down. You no dey settle the final bill until you don finish drinking, and even then na only once you go pay. Until that moment, you fit order unlimited drinks and the bartender go trust you because e dan know your face.

## In Context

I open Lightning channel with my friend so we fit send each other Bitcoin instantly.

What this means: You and another person lock Bitcoin for joint account (the channel). Now you fit send Bitcoin back and forth between each other instantly, unlimited times, with almost no fees. Na only when you close the channel, the final settlement go go for the blockchain.

## Why It Matters

Channels na wetin dey make Lightning Network possible. Dem dey scale Bitcoin transaction throughput from 7 transactions per second to thousands per second. Lightning dey essential for Bitcoin to work as everyday money, coffee, bills, remittances.
