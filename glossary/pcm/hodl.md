# HODL

**Category:** Community

## Plain English

Na strategy of buying Bitcoin and holding am long-term instead of selling during price fluctuations. The term come from misspell forum post wey say "I am hodling."

## Analogy

HODL na like buying land, then ignoring all the market noise about wetin the price go be next month. Instead of selling every time you hear negative news, you dey keep the land because you believe for its value long-term. Bitcoin HODLers dey do the same.

## In Context

I go HODL my Bitcoin for next five years.

What this means: You dey plan to buy Bitcoin and keep am no matter the price swings wey happen. You no dey try to time the market or profit from short-term trades, you dey hold for long-term value.

## Why It Matters

HODL na mindset wey dey common among Bitcoin believers. Historical data dey show say people wey buy Bitcoin and hold for years usually dey profit, even through multiple crashes. The opposite of HODL na panic selling during downturns, which dey lock in losses. HODL dey require patience and conviction.
