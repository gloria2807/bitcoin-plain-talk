# Atomic Swap

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

Na peer-to-peer exchange of two different cryptocurrencies or assets between two people without needing middleman or exchange.

## Analogy

Imagine say you get apples and your friend get oranges. You want to trade, but you no trust each other. You go market and pay broker to hold the apples while you dey receive the oranges. Atomic swap na the crypto version of trustless trade, Bitcoin code dey enforce the deal so both of you get wetin you agree, or nobody get anything.

## In Context

I do atomic swap so that I go fit exchange Bitcoin for Monero without using exchange.

What this means: You dey directly swap one coin for another with somebody else, using special smart contract wey dey ensure both sides dey honour the agreement. If one person no dey send their coin by deadline, both transactions dey cancel and your money go return.

## Why It Matters

Atomic swaps dey enable direct peer-to-peer trading without intermediaries. You no need to trust exchange with your funds, and you fit avoid fees and delays. Dem also dey increase privacy and decentralisation by removing need for centralised trading platforms.
