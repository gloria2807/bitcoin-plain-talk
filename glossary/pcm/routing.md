# Routing

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

Na process for the Lightning Network of finding the best path for payment to travel through multiple channels from sender to receiver.

## Analogy

Imagine one network of paths wey dey connect villages. You wan send message to somebody for distant village. E no get direct path, but you fit send the message through intermediary villages: A → B → C → D. Routing dey automatically figure out the fastest path. Lightning routing dey work the same way, e dey find the shortest chain of channels wey dey connect you to the person you wan pay.

## In Context

The Lightning Network route my payment through three channels to reach the recipient.

What this means: You no get direct channel open with the person you dey pay, so the network dey automatically find path through other channels. Your payment dey go through intermediary nodes, each of dem dey facilitate the transfer for exchange for small fee.

## Why It Matters

Routing na wetin dey make Lightning truly scalable. You no need to open channel with everybody you wan pay, just with few well-connect nodes, Routing dey find the path. This one dey similar to how the internet dey work: your data no dey go directly from you to the website, e dey route through multiple servers.
