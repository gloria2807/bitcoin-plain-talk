# Timelock

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

Na feature wey dey lock Bitcoin so you no fit spend am until specific time for the future or specific block height for the blockchain dey reach.

## Analogy

Think of time-locked safe wey only dey open for future date. You dey put money inside today, lock am, and nobody, not even you, fit access am until that date dey arrive. Bitcoin timelocks dey work the same way: one transaction fit dey create today but e no fit confirm until specific time pass.

## In Context

I use timelock to schedule payment two weeks from now.

What this means: You create transaction with instruction say e no fit confirm until two weeks don pass. Even if somebody try to broadcast am early, the network go dey reject am.

## Why It Matters

Timelocks dey essential for advanced Bitcoin features like payment channels (Lightning Network) and atomic swaps. Dem also dey let people set up inheritance or delayed payments. Timelocks dey add powerful flexibility to Bitcoin while maintaining security.
