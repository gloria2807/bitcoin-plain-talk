# Dollar-Cost Averaging

**Category:** Economy

## Plain English

A strategy of buying a fixed amount of Bitcoin at regular intervals, like weekly or monthly, regardless of the price, instead of trying to buy it all at once.

## Analogy

It's like filling a bucket with a garden hose over many small bursts instead of trying to dump the whole bucket of water in one go. Some bursts land when the ground is dry (prices are low) and some when it's already wet (prices are high), but over time the average works out reasonably well without you having to guess the perfect moment.

## In Context

*"I dollar-cost average $50 into Bitcoin every payday."*

**What this means:** Instead of trying to time the market and buy at the "best" price, the person buys the same dollar amount of Bitcoin on a set schedule, smoothing out the effect of price swings over time.

## Why It Matters

Timing the market perfectly is extremely difficult, even for professionals. Dollar-cost averaging removes the emotional guesswork of deciding when to buy, reducing the risk of putting a large sum in right before a price drop.
