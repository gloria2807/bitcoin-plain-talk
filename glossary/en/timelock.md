# Timelock

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

A feature that locks Bitcoin so it can't be spent until a specific time in the future or a specific block height on the blockchain is reached.

## Analogy

Think of a time-locked safe that only opens at a future date. You put money inside today, lock it, and nobody, not even you, can access it until that date arrives. Bitcoin timelocks work the same way: a transaction can be created today but can't be confirmed until a specific time passes.

## In Context

I used a timelock to schedule a payment two weeks from now.

What this means: You created a transaction with the instruction that it can't be confirmed until two weeks have passed. Even if someone tries to broadcast it early, the network will reject it.

## Why It Matters

Timelocks are essential for advanced Bitcoin features like payment channels (Lightning Network) and atomic swaps. They also allow people to set up inheritance or delayed payments. Timelocks add powerful flexibility to Bitcoin while maintaining security.
