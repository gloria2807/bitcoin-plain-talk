# Air-Gapped Wallet

**Category:** Security

## Plain English

A wallet whose private keys are stored on a device that has never been, and never will be, connected to the internet, protecting it from remote hacking.

## Analogy

Think of it like keeping your most valuable documents in a safe that's bolted inside a room with no doors or windows connecting it to the outside world. A thief on the internet simply has no path in, no matter how skilled they are, because there's no connection to exploit.

## In Context

*"I sign large transactions using an air-gapped wallet for extra security."*

**What this means:** This person keeps their private keys on a device that's never connected to Wi-Fi or the internet, transferring transaction data in and out using something like a QR code or SD card, so hackers have no remote way to reach the keys.

## Why It Matters

Most Bitcoin theft happens through remote hacks, malware, or phishing that exploits an internet connection. Going air-gapped eliminates that entire category of risk, though it requires more care and technical know-how to use correctly.
