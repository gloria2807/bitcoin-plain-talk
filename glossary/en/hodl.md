# HODL

**Category:** Community

## Plain English

A strategy of buying Bitcoin and holding it long-term instead of selling during price fluctuations. The term comes from a misspelled forum post that said "I am hodling".

## Analogy

HODL is like buying land, then ignoring all the market noise about what the price will be next month. Instead of selling every time you hear negative news, you keep the land because you believe in its value long-term. Bitcoin HODLers do the same.

## In Context

I'm going to HODL my Bitcoin for the next five years.

What this means:You're planning to buy Bitcoin and keep it regardless of price swings. You're not trying to time the market or profit from short-term trades, you're holding for long-term value.

## Why It Matters

HODL is a mindset common among Bitcoin believers. Historical data shows that people who bought Bitcoin and held for years usually profited, even through multiple crashes. The opposite of HODL is panic selling during downturns, which locks in losses. HODL requires patience and conviction.
