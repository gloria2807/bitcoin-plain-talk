# Hardware Wallet

**Category:** Security

## Plain English

A physical device, usually looking like a USB drive, that stores your Bitcoin private keys offline and signs transactions securely.

## Analogy

A hardware wallet is like a secure briefcase for your Bitcoin. You keep your money inside, and whenever you want to spend it, you unlock the case with a password. The money is sealed inside, even if a thief steals the briefcase, they can't get the money without the password.

## In Context

I use a hardware wallet like Ledger or Trezor to store my Bitcoin.

What this means: Your private keys are stored on a special device that's not connected to the internet. When you want to spend Bitcoin, you connect the device to a computer, approve the transaction on the device itself, and then it broadcasts the signed transaction to the network. Your keys never touch the internet.

## Why It Matters

Hardware wallets are the ideal balance between security and convenience. They're much safer than hot wallets because your private keys never touch an internet-connected device, but they're more convenient than paper wallets because you can easily spend Bitcoin. Most serious Bitcoin users keep their long-term savings in a hardware wallet.
