# Channel

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

A direct connection between two Lightning Network users that lets them send and receive Bitcoin back and forth instantly without touching the main blockchain.

## Analogy

A Lightning channel is like a tab at a pub. You and the bartender open a tab (a channel), then you can order drinks and the bartender marks it down. You don't settle the final bill until you're done drinking, and even then you only pay once. Until that moment, you can order unlimited drinks and the bartender trusts you because they know your face.

## In Context

I opened a Lightning channel with my friend so we can send each other Bitcoin instantly.

What this means: You and another person locked Bitcoin into a joint account (the channel). Now you can send Bitcoin back and forth between each other instantly, unlimited times, with almost no fees. Only when you close the channel does the final settlement go onto the blockchain.

## Why It Matters

Channels are what make Lightning Network possible. They scale Bitcoin's transaction throughput from 7 transactions per second to thousands per second. Lightning is essential for Bitcoin to work as everyday money, coffee, bills, remittances.
