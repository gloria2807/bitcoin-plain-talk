# Bear Market

**Category:** Economy

## Plain English

A sustained period when Bitcoin's price is falling and investor sentiment is pessimistic.

## Analogy

Picture a bear swiping its paw downward, the opposite motion of the bull. A bear market is like a long, cold winter: things look bleak, fewer people want to buy, and prices keep drifting lower, sometimes for many months at a time.

## In Context

*"Prices have been dropping for months, we're deep in a bear market."*

**What this means:** Bitcoin's price has been declining for an extended stretch, pessimism is widespread, and many people who bought at higher prices are sitting on losses.

## Why It Matters

Bear markets test conviction. Historically, some of the best long-term buying opportunities for Bitcoin have happened during bear markets, when prices are low and fear is high, but it takes discipline to buy or hold when everyone around you is discouraged.
