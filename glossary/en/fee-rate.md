# Fee Rate

**Category:** Transactions

## Plain English

The amount you pay per unit of transaction data (measured in satoshis per virtual byte), rather than a flat fee, determining how quickly miners are likely to include your transaction in a block.

## Analogy

Think of it like paying a courier by how much they charge per kilometer, not a flat delivery fee. A larger, heavier package (a bigger transaction) costs more overall, even at the same rate, because it takes up more space and effort to deliver.

## In Context

*"The mempool is congested right now, so I need a higher fee rate to get confirmed quickly."*

**What this means:** Because many people are competing to get their transactions into the next block, this person needs to offer a higher payment per byte of data to convince miners to prioritize their transaction over others willing to pay less.

## Why It Matters

Understanding fee rate helps you avoid overpaying during quiet periods and helps you know when to pay more to get confirmed quickly during busy periods. Most modern wallets estimate a reasonable fee rate automatically, but knowing what it means helps you make informed adjustments.
