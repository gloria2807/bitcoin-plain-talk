# Whale

**Category:** Community

## Plain English

An individual or entity that holds a very large amount of Bitcoin, enough that their buying or selling activity can noticeably move the market price.

## Analogy

Think of the difference between dropping a pebble into a pond versus dropping a boulder. A pebble barely causes a ripple, but a boulder sends waves across the whole surface. A whale's trades can send those bigger "waves" through the market that smaller holders simply can't create on their own.

## In Context

*"The price dropped suddenly, some people think a whale moved a huge amount of coins to an exchange."*

**What this means:** Onlookers noticed a very large transaction moving toward an exchange, historically often a sign that the holder might be preparing to sell, and are speculating that this large-scale activity might be connected to the sudden price movement.

## Why It Matters

Watching whale activity, often through public blockchain data, is a hobby for many traders trying to anticipate price movements, though correlation isn't always causation. It's also a reminder that Bitcoin ownership is quite concentrated among a relatively small number of large holders.
