# Chain Analysis

**Category:** Privacy

## Plain English

The practice of studying the public Bitcoin blockchain to try to identify patterns, link addresses together, and figure out who might be behind specific transactions.

## Analogy

Think of it like a detective studying a public ledger of every handshake that's ever happened in a city, noting who shook hands with whom and when, trying to piece together relationships and identities purely from the pattern of interactions, even though no names were ever written down.

## In Context

*"Exchanges use chain analysis tools to flag transactions linked to stolen funds."*

**What this means:** Companies and investigators use specialized software to trace the flow of bitcoin across the public blockchain, spotting patterns that might connect an address to a known exchange, a criminal wallet, or another identifiable entity.

## Why It Matters

Bitcoin's blockchain is fully public and permanent, meaning every transaction can be studied forever. Chain analysis is used both defensively, to fight fraud and money laundering, and in ways that can erode ordinary users' privacy, which is why techniques like Coinjoin exist.
