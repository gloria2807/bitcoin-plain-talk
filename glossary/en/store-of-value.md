# Store of Value

**Category:** Economy

## Plain English

An asset that reliably holds or increases its purchasing power over time, instead of losing value the way cash sitting under a mattress does.

## Analogy

Think of gold that a family has kept for generations. It doesn't produce income like a rental property, but it doesn't rot, rust, or get eaten by inflation either, it just sits there, holding its worth. Many Bitcoin supporters describe Bitcoin as "digital gold" for the same reason: its fixed supply makes it resistant to being devalued over time.

## In Context

*"I hold some Bitcoin as a store of value, not to spend day-to-day."*

**What this means:** The person is treating their Bitcoin like a long-term savings vehicle, similar to gold or property, rather than using it for everyday purchases.

## Why It Matters

Whether Bitcoin is truly a good store of value is debated, its price is far more volatile than gold in the short term. But its capped supply of 21 million coins and resistance to censorship or seizure are the core reasons people make the comparison.
