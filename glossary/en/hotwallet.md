# Hot Wallet

**Category:** Security

## Plain English

A Bitcoin wallet connected to the internet, designed for frequent spending and convenience rather than maximum security.

## Analogy

A hot wallet is like having money in your pocke, accessible and easy to spend, but also more exposed to theft. A cold wallet is like having money locked in a safe at home. Most people use both: keep some money in their pocket for daily use, and the rest at home for safekeeping.

## In Context

I keep a small amount of Bitcoin in a hot wallet on my phone for daily purchases.

What this means: You're storing Bitcoin in a wallet that's connected to the internet, making it quick to send from anywhere. But because it's online, it has more security risks than offline wallets.

## Why It Matters

Hot wallets are necessary for active Bitcoin users. You need one to buy things and receive payments. But security experts recommend only keeping the amount you need for short-term use in a hot wallet, and storing the rest in cold storage. This balances convenience with security.
