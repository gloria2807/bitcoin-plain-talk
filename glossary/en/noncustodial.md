# Non-Custodial

**Category:** Security

## Plain English

A Bitcoin wallet where you hold your own private keys and have complete control of your funds, nobody else can access or freeze your Bitcoin.

## Analogy

A non-custodial wallet is like keeping cash at home in a safe. You control everything. Nobody can lock you out, freeze your funds, or disappear with your money. But you're also responsible for the safety of the safe and the keys.

## In Context

I use a non-custodial wallet, I hold all my private keys myself.

What this means: You downloaded a wallet app (like BlueWallet, Sparrow, or Ledger), generated a private key, and now you alone have the private key. No company is involved. You can send and receive Bitcoin completely independently.

## Why It Matters

Non-custodial is Bitcoin's core value proposition: true ownership and control. No government can freeze your account. No company can lock you out. No censorship is possible. If you lose the private key, you lose the Bitcoin forever, but that's the tradeoff for complete freedom. This is why serious Bitcoin users prefer non-custodial wallets.
