# Stealth Address

**Category:** Privacy

## Plain English

A privacy technique where a sender uses a public address to generate a brand-new, one-time address for each payment, so outside observers can't link multiple payments to the same recipient.

## Analogy

Imagine a business that gives out one single publicly listed phone number, but behind the scenes, every call gets automatically forwarded to a different, unique, one-time mailbox. Anyone who intercepts the mail can't tell that all these different mailboxes belong to the same business, even though everyone calls the same public number to reach it.

## In Context

*"That wallet supports stealth addresses so recipients don't have to share a new address for every payment."*

**What this means:** The recipient can publish one address publicly, but every payment sent to it is automatically directed to a fresh, unique address behind the scenes, hiding the link between multiple payments to the same person.

## Why It Matters

Stealth addresses solve a real usability problem: needing to generate and share a new address for every single payment. They aim to give both privacy and convenience at the same time, though they've seen more adoption in other cryptocurrencies than in Bitcoin itself.
