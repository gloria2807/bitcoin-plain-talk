# Passphrase

**Category:** Security

## Plain English

An optional extra word or phrase you add on top of your seed phrase, often called the "25th word," which creates an entirely different wallet that can't be accessed with the seed phrase alone.

## Analogy

Think of your seed phrase as the key to a hidden room, and the passphrase as a second, secret combination lock on a door inside that room that leads to an even more hidden room. Someone who finds the key alone can open the first room, but without the combination, the second room, and whatever is in it, stays completely inaccessible.

## In Context

*"Even if someone finds my seed phrase, they can't access my funds without my passphrase."*

**What this means:** This person added an extra passphrase to their wallet, so their real funds sit behind a wallet that only appears if both the seed phrase and the exact passphrase are entered together. The seed phrase alone opens a different, empty-looking wallet.

## Why It Matters

A passphrase adds a powerful extra layer of protection against someone finding your physical seed phrase backup, but it comes with real risk: if you forget your exact passphrase, even one character off, your funds are permanently unrecoverable, since there's no way to reset it.
