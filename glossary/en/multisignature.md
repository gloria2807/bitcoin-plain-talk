# Multi-signature

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

A wallet that requires multiple private keys to approve a transaction, instead of just one. Often written as "multisig."

## Analogy

Think of a safe at a bank that needs three keys to open. Even if someone steals one key, they still can't open the safe without the other two. Multi-signature works the same way, you might set it up so that three out of five people must approve a transaction before it goes through.

## In Context

We set up a 2-of-3 multisig wallet for our Bitcoin savings.

What this means: The wallet needs signatures from 2 out of the 3 owners before any Bitcoin can be sent. If one person's key is stolen, the funds are still safe because the thief needs at least one other owner's approval.

## Why It Matters

Multi-signature is one of the best security practices for Bitcoin. It protects against theft (no single point of failure) and prevents accidental mistakes (requires consensus). Families, companies, and groups often use multisig to secure large amounts of Bitcoin.
