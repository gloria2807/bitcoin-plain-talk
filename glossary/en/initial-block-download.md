# Initial Block Download

**Category:** Network

## Plain English

The process a new full node goes through when it first starts up, downloading and verifying the entire history of the Bitcoin blockchain from other nodes on the network.

## Analogy

Imagine moving to a new city and wanting to catch up on every local newspaper ever printed, so you can independently verify everything you've heard about the town's history rather than just trusting someone's summary. That's what a new node does: it reads and checks every block since the very beginning, rather than trusting anyone else's word for it.

## In Context

*"My node is still doing its initial block download, it'll take a day or two."*

**What this means:** The person just set up a new full node, and it's currently downloading and independently verifying every block in Bitcoin's history, a process that can take from several hours to a couple of days depending on hardware and internet speed.

## Why It Matters

Initial block download is what allows a node to verify Bitcoin's entire history for itself, rather than trusting someone else's claims about what happened. This is the foundation of Bitcoin's "don't trust, verify" principle.
