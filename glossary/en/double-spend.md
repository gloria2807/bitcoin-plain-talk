# Double-Spend

**Category:** Transactions

## Plain English

An attempt to spend the same bitcoin twice, once was the core problem Bitcoin was invented to solve, since digital files can normally be copied endlessly.

## Analogy

Imagine trying to hand the same physical dollar bill to two different people at the same time, claiming to pay both of them with it. With physical cash, that's impossible, you only have one bill. Bitcoin needed a way to make digital money behave the same way, so it can't be copy-pasted and spent twice.

## In Context

*"Bitcoin's blockchain prevents double-spending by having the whole network agree on transaction order."*

**What this means:** Once a transaction is confirmed and buried under enough blocks, the entire network has collectively agreed it happened, making it computationally impractical for someone to secretly spend that same bitcoin again elsewhere.

## Why It Matters

Solving the double-spend problem without a central authority, like a bank, was Bitcoin's core innovation. It's why waiting for confirmations matters: an unconfirmed transaction could theoretically still be double-spent before the network settles on which version is valid.
