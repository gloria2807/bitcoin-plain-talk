# Market Cap

**Category:** Economy

## Plain English

The total value of all bitcoin in existence, calculated by multiplying the current price of one bitcoin by the total number of coins that have been mined.

## Analogy

If a single share of a company is worth $10 and there are one million shares, the company is valued at $10 million. Market cap for Bitcoin works the same way: take the price of one bitcoin, multiply it by how many bitcoin exist, and you get the total value of the entire network.

## In Context

*"Bitcoin's market cap crossed a trillion dollars."*

**What this means:** The combined value of every bitcoin ever mined, at the current market price, added up to more than a trillion dollars. This is a common way to compare Bitcoin's overall size to other assets like gold, stocks, or other cryptocurrencies.

## Why It Matters

Market cap gives a sense of scale and adoption, but it can be misleading. A small amount of buying or selling can swing the price and market cap dramatically if trading volume is low, so market cap alone doesn't tell you how easily that value could actually be converted to cash.
