# Descriptor

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

A standardized text string that describes exactly how a wallet's addresses are generated and spent, including the keys involved and the script type.

## Analogy

Think of it like a precise blueprint for a house rather than just the address of one room. A single house address tells you where one room is, but a blueprint tells you the whole structure, how every room connects and what it's made of. A descriptor captures the full structure of how a wallet's funds are locked and unlocked, not just one address.

## In Context

*"Back up your wallet descriptor, not just your seed phrase, especially for multisig setups."*

**What this means:** For more complex wallets, especially ones needing multiple signatures, just having the seed phrase isn't always enough to restore funds correctly. The descriptor records the full setup, so pairing it with your backup ensures your wallet can be reconstructed exactly.

## Why It Matters

Descriptors are becoming the standard way wallets describe themselves, replacing older, less precise methods. They reduce the risk of restoration errors, especially for multisignature or script-based wallets where a seed phrase alone doesn't capture the whole picture.
