# Transaction

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

A record of Bitcoin being sent from one wallet to another. Every transaction is broadcast to the network, verified by nodes, and permanently stored on the blockchain.

## Analogy

Think of a receipt from a market. When you buy something, you get a piece of paper that says who paid what, when, and where. A Bitcoin transaction is the same, it's the proof that someone sent Bitcoin to someone else at a specific time.

## In Context

My transaction has three confirmations.

What this means: A transaction you sent has been recorded in three separate blocks on the blockchain. The more confirmations, the more permanent and irreversible that transaction becomes.

## Why It Matters

Transactions are what Bitcoin is built for, the ability to move value from one person to another. Every transaction is transparent, irreversible once confirmed, and cannot be censored. Once a transaction gets enough confirmations, it's final.
