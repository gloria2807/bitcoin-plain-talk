# FOMO

**Category:** Community

## Plain English

Short for "Fear Of Missing Out," the anxious feeling that drives people to buy Bitcoin impulsively during a fast price rise, worried they'll miss further gains if they don't act immediately.

## Analogy

Imagine seeing a long line outside a shop and rushing to join it, not because you know what they're selling, but simply because everyone else seems to want it. FOMO in Bitcoin works the same way: rising prices and excited headlines can pull people into buying without a clear plan, purely out of fear of being left behind.

## In Context

*"Don't let FOMO push you into buying more than you can afford during a price spike."*

**What this means:** The person is warning against making a rushed, emotionally driven purchase decision just because prices are climbing quickly and it feels like everyone else is profiting.

## Why It Matters

FOMO-driven buying often happens near short-term price peaks, right before a pullback, leading to poor entry points and regret. Recognizing FOMO as an emotional reaction, rather than a sound strategy, helps people stick to calmer approaches like dollar-cost averaging.
