# Silent Payments

**Category:** Privacy

## Plain English

A proposed Bitcoin privacy standard that lets someone publish a single reusable address while every payment sent to it lands on a unique, unlinkable address on the blockchain, without any interaction needed from the recipient.

## Analogy

Think of it like a mailbox that automatically relabels itself with a brand-new, random address every time a letter is dropped in, so from the outside, nobody can tell that all those different-looking letters ended up at the very same house.

## In Context

*"Silent payments would let me share one address publicly without exposing my transaction history."*

**What this means:** Instead of needing to generate and distribute a new address for each payment manually, this person could share a single public address, and thanks to some clever cryptography, every incoming payment would still land on a fresh, unlinkable on-chain address.

## Why It Matters

Address reuse is one of the biggest, most common privacy leaks in Bitcoin today. Silent payments aim to solve it without requiring extra interaction between sender and receiver, making strong privacy the easy default rather than an extra step.
