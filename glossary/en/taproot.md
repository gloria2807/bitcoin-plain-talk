# Taproot

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

A 2021 upgrade to Bitcoin that makes transactions more private, cheaper, and enables more complex smart contracts, without being visible on the blockchain.

## Analogy

Before Taproot, every detail of a transaction was obvious on the blockchain, like everyone could see inside your envelope before you sent it. Taproot encrypts those details, so only the sender and receiver know what the transaction actually contains. To the rest of the network, it looks like a simple transaction.

## In Context

Taproot addresses start with 'bc1p', they're more private than regular addresses.

What this means: When you send to a Taproot address, the network knows a transaction happened, but it can't see the details. This makes Bitcoin more private while also reducing transaction size and fees.

## Why It Matters

Taproot is one of Bitcoin's most significant upgrades since SegWit. It improved privacy without requiring extra space on the blockchain. It also enabled more sophisticated Bitcoin contracts, like atomic swaps and complex payment channels, that were previously impossible or inefficient.
