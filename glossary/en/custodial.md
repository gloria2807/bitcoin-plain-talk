# Custodial

**Category:** Security

## Plain English

A Bitcoin service where a third party (like an exchange or bank) holds your private keys and controls your Bitcoin on your behalf.

## Analogy

A custodial wallet is like keeping money in a bank. You don't hold the money yourself, the bank does. You trust the bank to give your money back when you ask. If the bank gets hacked or goes out of business, your money might be at risk.

## In Context

I keep my Bitcoin on an exchange — that's a custodial setup.

What this means: Coinbase, Kraken, and other exchanges are custodial services. They hold your Bitcoin and your private keys. You access your funds through their website or app, but you don't actually control the private keys.

## Why It Matters

Custodial services are convenient for beginners, but they sacrifice one of Bitcoin's core benefits: self-sovereignty. You're trusting a company to secure your money and not disappear. There's a famous saying in Bitcoin: "Not your keys, not your coins." If you want full control and security of your Bitcoin, move it to a non-custodial wallet.
