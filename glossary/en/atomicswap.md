# Atomic Swap

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

A peer-to-peer exchange of two different cryptocurrencies or assets between two people without needing a middleman or exchange.

## Analogy

Imagine you have apples and your friend has oranges. You want to trade, but you don't trust each other. You go to the market and pay a broker to hold the apples while receiving the oranges. An atomic swap is the crypto version of a trustless trade, Bitcoin's code enforces the deal so both of you get what you agreed to, or neither of you gets anything.

## In Context

I did an atomic swap to exchange Bitcoin for Monero without using an exchange.

What this means: You directly swapped one coin for another with someone else, using a special smart contract that ensures both sides honour the agreement. If one person doesn't send their coin by the deadline, both transactions are cancelled and your money is returned.

## Why It Matters

Atomic swaps enable direct peer-to-peer trading without intermediaries. You don't have to trust an exchange with your funds, and you avoid fees and delays. They also increase privacy and decentralisation by removing the need for centralised trading platforms.
