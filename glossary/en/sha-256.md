# SHA-256

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

The specific cryptographic hash function Bitcoin uses to secure transactions and blocks, turning any input into a unique, fixed-length string of characters.

## Analogy

Think of SHA-256 like a industrial paper shredder that always produces a fixed-size pile of confetti, no matter if you feed it one page or a thousand pages. Feed it the same page twice and you always get the exact same pile. Change even one letter on the page and the resulting confetti pile looks completely different. You can't reconstruct the page from the confetti either.

## In Context

*"Bitcoin mining involves running SHA-256 hashing over and over."*

**What this means:** Miners are repeatedly feeding block data through the SHA-256 function, tweaking a small value each time, searching for an output that meets the network's difficulty target.

## Why It Matters

SHA-256 is the mathematical backbone of Bitcoin's security. It's what makes mining require real computational work, what secures the link between blocks in the blockchain, and what makes tampering with past transactions practically impossible.
