# Cypherpunk

**Category:** Community

## Plain English

A member of a movement, dating back to the late 1980s, that advocated for using cryptography and privacy-preserving technology to protect individual freedom from governments and corporations.

## Analogy

Think of cypherpunks like a group of locksmiths who believed everyone deserved the tools to lock their own doors, rather than relying on a landlord who could let themselves in whenever they wanted. They believed strong, personal cryptography, not permission from an authority, was the real path to privacy and freedom.

## In Context

*"Satoshi Nakamoto first announced Bitcoin on a cypherpunk mailing list."*

**What this means:** Bitcoin wasn't announced through a press release or a company, it was shared on an email list frequented by cypherpunks, people already deeply interested in cryptography, privacy, and building systems that didn't require trusting a central authority.

## Why It Matters

Bitcoin didn't appear out of nowhere, it grew directly out of decades of cypherpunk ideas and earlier failed attempts at digital cash. Understanding this history helps explain why Bitcoin prioritizes decentralization, privacy, and resistance to censorship so heavily.
