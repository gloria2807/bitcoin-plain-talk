# Altcoin

**Category:** Community

## Plain English

Any cryptocurrency other than Bitcoin. The term literally means "alternative coin".

## Analogy

If Bitcoin is dollars, altcoins are other currencies like euros, yen, or Nigerian naira. Some might be more useful for specific purposes, some might be scams, but they're all alternatives to Bitcoin.

## In Context

Ethereum and Litecoin are popular altcoins.

What this means: Bitcoin was the first cryptocurrency. Every other coin, Ethereum, Monero, Litecoin, Dogecoin, etc. is considered an altcoin. Some altcoins have real use cases, while others are purely speculative or scams.

## Why It Matters

Understanding altcoins is important because the crypto market is full of them. While Bitcoin focuses on being sound money, some altcoins aim to do other things, run programs, enable privacy, serve specific communities. However, be cautious: many altcoins are scams designed to take your money. Bitcoin remains the most secure, most decentralised, and most trusted cryptocurrency.
