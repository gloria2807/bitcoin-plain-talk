# Paper Wallet

**Category:** Security

## Plain English

A Bitcoin wallet stored entirely on paper, your private key and public address are printed, making it offline and immune to hacking.

## Analogy

A paper wallet is like writing down your house key and address on a piece of paper and storing it in a safe place. No computer can hack it because it's not digital. But if you lose the paper or someone steals it, your funds are gone.

## In Context

I generated a paper wallet, printed it, and stored it in a safe deposit box for long-term savings.

What this means:You used a tool to generate a private key, printed both the key and address on paper, destroyed the digital copy, and now you have a completely offline Bitcoin storage method.

## Why It Matters

Paper wallets are excellent for large amounts of Bitcoin you plan to hold for years. Because they're offline, they're one of the safest ways to store Bitcoin, no hacking, no malware, no loss of access. The downside is that spending takes extra work: you must import the private key into a digital wallet before you can send Bitcoin.
