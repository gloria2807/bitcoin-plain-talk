# Coinbase Transaction

**Category:** Mining

## Plain English

The special first transaction in every block, created by the miner, which is how new bitcoin is minted and paid out as the block reward.

## Analogy

Think of it like the receipt a bakery writes for itself when it bakes a fresh batch of bread to sell, there's no customer paying for that specific transaction, it's simply the bakery recording that new bread now exists and belongs to it. The coinbase transaction is Bitcoin's way of recording that new coins have been created and awarded to the miner.

## In Context

*"The coinbase transaction paid the miner 3.125 bitcoin plus the fees from all the other transactions in the block."*

**What this means:** The miner who successfully mined that block received a payout combining the fixed block reward and the total transaction fees from everyone whose transactions were included in that block.

## Why It Matters

Every single bitcoin that exists was created through a coinbase transaction at some point. It's also worth noting: this "coinbase" has nothing to do with the cryptocurrency exchange of the same name, the term predates the company.
