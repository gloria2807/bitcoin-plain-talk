# Block Reward

**Category:** Mining

## Plain English

The newly created bitcoin a miner receives for successfully mining a block, currently set at a fixed amount that cuts in half roughly every four years.

## Analogy

Think of it like a prize given to whoever finishes a very difficult puzzle race first, roughly every ten minutes, a new race begins, and the winner gets a fresh reward. Over time, the prize amount is deliberately reduced on a set schedule, making the total number of prizes ever given out limited.

## In Context

*"The block reward dropped from 6.25 to 3.125 bitcoin after the halving."*

**What this means:** Following a scheduled halving event, miners now receive half as much newly created bitcoin for each block they successfully mine compared to before.

## Why It Matters

The block reward is how new bitcoin enters circulation, and its scheduled reduction is what makes Bitcoin's total supply predictably capped at 21 million. It also means miners increasingly rely on transaction fees for revenue as the reward shrinks over time.
