# Watch-Only Wallet

**Category:** Security

## Plain English

A wallet that can see balances and transaction history for a set of addresses, but holds no private keys and therefore cannot spend or move any funds.

## Analogy

Think of it like being able to look through the window of a vault and count exactly how much gold is inside, without having a key that lets you open the door. You can monitor everything, but you can't touch anything.

## In Context

*"I set up a watch-only wallet on my phone so I can check my balance without exposing my keys."*

**What this means:** This person imported only the public information (like an extended public key) into their phone wallet, letting them check balances and incoming transactions on the go, while their private keys stay safely offline where they can't be stolen from the phone.

## Why It Matters

Watch-only wallets let people monitor their cold storage holdings conveniently from an everyday device, without ever exposing the private keys that could actually move the funds, striking a useful balance between convenience and security.
