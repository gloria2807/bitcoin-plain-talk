# Bull Market

**Category:** Economy

## Plain English

A sustained period when Bitcoin's price is rising and investor confidence is high.

## Analogy

Think of a bull thrusting its horns upward, that's the image behind the term. A bull market is like a rising tide that lifts most boats: optimism spreads, more people want to buy, and prices tend to climb higher over weeks or months.

## In Context

*"We're in a bull market, everyone's talking about Bitcoin again."*

**What this means:** Bitcoin's price has been climbing for a while, media coverage has picked up, and more people are entering the market because they're excited about further gains.

## Why It Matters

Bull markets can create excitement and fast profits, but they can also encourage people to buy impulsively near price peaks out of fear of missing out. Recognizing a bull market for what it is, a phase, not a permanent state, helps people make calmer decisions instead of chasing hype.
