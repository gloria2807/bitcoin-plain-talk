# Dust

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

A very small amount of bitcoin, so small that the fee needed to spend it can cost more than the amount itself is worth.

## Analogy

Imagine finding a single penny stuck to the sidewalk. Technically it's money, but bending down to pick it up and carry it to the bank might cost you more in time and effort than the penny is worth. Dust in a Bitcoin wallet works the same way, it's real value, but often not worth spending on its own.

## In Context

*"My wallet has some dust left over from an old transaction I can barely spend."*

**What this means:** The person has a leftover UTXO so tiny that including it in a new transaction would push the network fee above the value of the dust itself, making it impractical to move on its own.

## Why It Matters

Understanding dust helps explain why wallets sometimes show a balance that seems slightly off from what's usable, and why some transactions get flagged as "dusting attacks," where someone sends tiny amounts to many addresses to try to link them together for tracking purposes.
