# Whitepaper

**Category:** Community

## Plain English

The original nine-page document titled "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System," published by Satoshi Nakamoto in October 2008, describing how Bitcoin would work.

## Analogy

Think of it like the founding blueprint for a building that's since grown into a massive city. The blueprint itself is short and simple, but everything built on top of it over the following years traces its design back to those original few pages.

## In Context

*"Have you actually read the Bitcoin whitepaper?"*

**What this means:** The question is asking whether the person has gone back to the original source material, Satoshi Nakamoto's own explanation of Bitcoin's design, rather than relying only on secondhand summaries or opinions from other people.

## Why It Matters

The whitepaper is considered essential reading in the Bitcoin community because it lays out, in the creator's own words, the core problems Bitcoin solves and how it does so. It remains surprisingly readable even for non-technical people and is often the first "real" document newcomers are pointed to.
