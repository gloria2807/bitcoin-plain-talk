# Coinjoin

**Category:** Privacy

## Plain English

A method where multiple people combine their Bitcoin transactions together to increase privacy, making it harder to track who sent money to whom.

## Analogy

Imagine ten people at a market, each with money to send to a different person. Instead of sending individually (where everyone can see who's paying whom), they put all their money in a pot, mix it up, and then redistribute it to the destinations. Now nobody can tell which source paid which destination.

## In Context

I used Coinjoin to increase the privacy of my transaction.

What this means: You sent your Bitcoin through a service that combines it with other people's Bitcoin in a single transaction, then distributes the outputs. An outside observer can see the transaction happened, but can't determine which input corresponds to which output.

## Why It Matters

Bitcoin is pseudonymous but not anonymous, transactions are visible on the blockchain. Someone determined enough could track your spending. Coinjoin makes tracking much harder. Privacy advocates use Coinjoin to reclaim financial privacy, but be aware that some exchanges and services look down on CoinJoin because it increases suspicion.
