# Sidechain

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

A separate blockchain that runs alongside Bitcoin and is connected to it, allowing bitcoin to be moved over, used under different rules, and moved back later.

## Analogy

Think of it like a side street off a main highway. The main highway (Bitcoin) has strict, well-established traffic rules that are slow to change. A side street connected to it might allow different speed limits or experimental traffic patterns, useful for trying new things, while cars can still drive back onto the main highway whenever they want.

## In Context

*"That wallet lets you move Bitcoin onto a sidechain for faster, cheaper transactions."*

**What this means:** The user can transfer their bitcoin to a connected sidechain, where it might move faster or support extra features not available on the main Bitcoin network, and later move it back to the main chain.

## Why It Matters

Sidechains let developers experiment with new features and faster transactions without changing Bitcoin's own base-layer rules, which are intentionally conservative to protect security. They come with their own trust and security trade-offs, since moving bitcoin onto a sidechain usually means trusting a different set of validators.
