# BIP

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

Short for Bitcoin Improvement Proposal. A formal document that describes a suggested upgrade or change to Bitcoin's rules or standards.

## Analogy

Think of a BIP as a petition to improve your city. Someone writes down an idea for improvement, presents it to the community, discusses it, and if enough people agree, it gets implemented. Bitcoin improvement proposals work the same way, anyone can write one, but it only becomes reality if the community (miners, developers, nodes) accepts it.

## In Context

SegWit was introduced as BIP 141.

What this means:  Different upgrades are assigned different BIP numbers. BIP 141 was the official proposal for SegWit, BIP 32 was for hierarchical deterministic wallets, etc. Developers and miners reference BIPs to understand what change is being discussed.

## Why It Matters

BIPs are how Bitcoin evolves without a central authority. Anyone can propose one, but adoption is voluntary. This keeps Bitcoin decentralised, no single person can force a change on the entire network. The BIP process is transparent and community-driven.
