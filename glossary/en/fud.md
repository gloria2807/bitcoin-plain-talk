# FUD

**Category:** Community

## Plain English

Short for "Fear, Uncertainty, and Doubt," a term used to describe negative, often exaggerated or misleading, information spread about Bitcoin to scare people away from it.

## Analogy

Think of it like a rumor spread around a neighborhood that a perfectly safe park is dangerous, based on little or no real evidence, just to keep people from going there. Whether the fear is justified or not, the effect is the same: people become anxious and stay away.

## In Context

*"Don't sell just because of some FUD you read online, look at the actual facts first."*

**What this means:** The person is advising against making a hasty decision based on alarming but possibly exaggerated or false claims, and instead recommending a closer, more careful look at what's actually true.

## Why It Matters

FUD can come from genuine concerns worth investigating, or from bad actors trying to manipulate prices or public opinion. Learning to tell the difference, checking sources and facts rather than reacting emotionally, is an important skill for anyone navigating Bitcoin news.
