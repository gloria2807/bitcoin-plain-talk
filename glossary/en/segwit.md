# SegWit

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

Short for Segregated Witness. It's a 2017 upgrade to Bitcoin that made transactions smaller and cheaper by reorganising how transaction data is stored.

## Analogy

Imagine a bus with assigned seats. Before SegWit, every passenger (transaction) carried their luggage on their lap, taking up seat space. SegWit said move all the luggage to the boot of the bus, suddenly the same bus can carry more passengers. Transactions became lighter, more could fit in each block, and fees dropped as a result.

## In Context

Make sure you're sending to a SegWit address to save on fees.

SegWit addresses (which usually start with "bc1") use the upgraded transaction format, so they take up less space in a block and therefore cost less in fees. If you send to a legacy address instead, you pay slightly more.

## Why It Matters

SegWit was one of Bitcoin's most significant upgrades. It fixed a long-standing technical bug called transaction malleability, reduced fees, and laid the groundwork for the Lightning Network. It was activated as a soft fork, meaning nobody was forced to upgrade, but almost everyone eventually did because of the benefits.
