# Sponsoring Bitcoin Plain Talk 💖

Thank you for considering financial support for Bitcoin Plain Talk. Sponsorship helps us make clear, culturally relevant Bitcoin education available in more African languages.

## What Sponsorship Supports

Funding may be used for:

- Translator and language-reviewer stipends
- Bitcoin technical review of glossary entries
- Hosting, domains, and project infrastructure
- Accessibility, audio, and visual learning resources
- Community translation sessions and contributor onboarding
- Dataset preparation, documentation, and open-source tooling

## How to Sponsor

Bitcoin Plain Talk does not currently publish a payment address or funding platform in this repository. To discuss sponsorship:

1. Open a GitHub issue with the title `Sponsorship inquiry: <your name or organization>`.
2. Describe the type of support you would like to provide.
3. A maintainer will confirm the official payment method and next steps through a project-owned channel.

For your safety, do not send funds to an address shared only in an unsolicited direct message or comment. Verify payment details with a maintainer through the repository before sending. No legitimate maintainer will ask for your seed phrase or private keys.

## Ways to Support the Project

### One-Time Support

Help fund a defined need, such as reviewing a set of translations, improving documentation, or covering infrastructure costs.

### Recurring Support

Provide steady funding for ongoing translation, review, maintenance, and hosting work.

### Sponsored Milestones

Fund a clearly scoped outcome, for example:

- Completing and reviewing a glossary in one language
- Adding audio explanations for a group of terms
- Improving contributor or translation tooling
- Preparing an open dataset release

Milestones should be agreed with maintainers before funds are sent. The agreement should state the scope, expected output, timeline, reporting method, and any restrictions on the funds.

### In-Kind Support

Useful non-cash contributions can include hosting credits, event space, accessibility expertise, translation tools, design support, and community outreach. Please open an issue before providing a service so maintainers can confirm that it fits a current project need.

## Funding Principles

Sponsorship does not buy control over editorial decisions, technical conclusions, contributor reviews, or the order in which pull requests are merged. Project content must remain accurate, beginner-friendly, culturally respectful, and consistent with the project's open-source mission.

Sponsors may suggest a language, feature, or milestone to support, but maintainers retain final responsibility for scope, quality, safety, and acceptance.

## Transparency and Recognition

Before sponsoring, agree with maintainers on:

- Whether the sponsor will be named publicly or remain anonymous
- How the support may be acknowledged
- Whether progress or spending reports are required
- Whether the contribution is unrestricted or tied to an approved milestone

Public recognition will never imply that a sponsor endorses every project statement or that the project endorses a sponsor's products or services.

## Organizations and Grants

Organizations that need an invoice, grant proposal, memorandum of understanding, tax documentation, or detailed budget should include those requirements in their sponsorship inquiry. Maintainers will confirm what the project can provide before any commitment is made.

## Questions

Open a GitHub issue for sponsorship questions. Please do not include private financial, identity, or banking information in a public issue; a maintainer can provide an appropriate private channel if needed.

Thank you for helping make Bitcoin knowledge easier to understand in the languages people use every day.
